Shipping and returns

Orders leave Kathmandu within 3 business days and arrive by insured DHL Express in 5 to 8 days. Orders over $150 ship free. Return any item within 30 days of delivery for a refund.

Questions

Are the paintings really hand-painted?

Yes. Each thangka and mandala is painted by one artist and ships with a signed certificate.

Will I pay customs duties?

Import duties depend on your country. We declare the true value, and you pay any duty on delivery.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Every parcel is insured. Send a photo within 7 days and we will replace the item or refund you.

Can I get a custom size or deity?

Yes, for thangkas. Email us your request and the painter will quote a price and timeline.
